Hokayantra Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Hokayantra app ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. Hokayantra is developed by Piyush Patel.

Summary

  • You don't need an account, and we don't run any servers. We never receive your data.
  • Everything you enter in the app (finances, tasks, habits, journal, workouts and settings) stays on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads.
  • If you turn on City/State display, your coordinates are sent to your phone's built-in place-name service (Google on Android, Apple on iOS) to look up the place name.

Information stored only on your device

The following is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Settings: theme, language, compass preferences and pinned tools.
  • MoneyFlow: transactions, budgets, goals, bills, subscriptions and an optional PIN.
  • FocusFlow: tasks, habits, journal and mood entries, study and exam plans, and focus session history.
  • Workouts and timers: workout logs, exercise history, interval presets and session history.

This data stays on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. If you use a backup or export feature, the file goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Device permissions

Permission Used for Where the data goes
Location True North declination, coordinates, altitude and City/State Coordinates are used on your device. Only if City/State display is on are they sent to your platform's place-name service (Google or Apple) to get a place name.
Camera Magnifier and flashlight The live preview is shown on screen only. Images are not saved or sent anywhere.
Motion and magnetic sensors Compass, level, angle meter, metal detector and other tools Processed on your device only.

You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your device settings. Only the features that need it will stop working.

Advertising

The app shows banner and full-screen ads served by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA)
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and app version
  • how you interact with ads

Google uses this information as described in its policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings. If you don't consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use limited data.
  • iPhone: you will be asked whether the app may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA is not shared.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Children

Hokayantra is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the DPDP Act in India), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its use.

  • Data in the app: it is only on your device, so you control it directly. Delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Advertising data: this is held by Google. You can manage it through the ad privacy settings above and at myadcenter.google.com.

We don't sell your personal information.

Security

App data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't access. MoneyFlow can be protected with a PIN. On Android, its screen is also hidden from screenshots and the recent apps view while the PIN is on.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
